Targeted testing

  • Early acceleration (first steps, below about 4 yd/s) — timed starts over 0–5 and 0–10 yd with split timing, at least two repetitions, recorded from before the first movement.
  • Late acceleration and high speed (above about 6 yd/s) — a longer sprint or flying 20-yard segment, because 40 yards can end before top speed is reached.
  • Mid-sprint speed development (about 4 to 8 yd/s) — rolling-start (flying) 10-yard segments entered at a controlled speed, so the 4 → 8 yd/s band is measured directly.
  • Flying sprint (for sustained high-speed running, 26.7 per 100 eligible player-plays): a flying 20-yd segment after a 20–30-yd build-up, so top speed is reached and held inside the recording.
  • Rolling-start acceleration (for acceleration from intermediate speed, 6.8 per 100 eligible player-plays): enter a 10-yd zone at a controlled 4 yd/s and accelerate maximally; measures the 4 → 8 yd/s band from a rolling start.
  • WR movement sequence (for position-specific movement sequence, combines the demands above): release → stem at intermediate speed → brake → break at 45–90° → re-accelerate; tracked at ≥ 10 Hz with the same band and braking rules as the NFL atlas.
  • A proposed test is a candidate way to measure a demand the forty does not describe. Its value for evaluating NFL prospects has not been established and would need prospective evidence.
