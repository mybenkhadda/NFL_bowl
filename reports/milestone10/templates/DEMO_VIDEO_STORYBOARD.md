**Export.** 1920 × 1080, H.264, 30 fps, ≤ 120 s, AAC 192 kbps; captions burned in from the voiceover column (accessibility). File name `cil_demo_v1.mp4`; keep the raw screen recording.

**Fallbacks.** If the replay stutters, set playback to 0.5× and narrate over it. If the gap position differs from the script after 10C runs, use the rendered token above (it is the rule-selected position). If the same-forty pair is absent in a smoke mart, record from a full mart only.

**Claims to avoid on camera.** "better player", "will succeed", "draft grade", "predicts", "hidden star", any probability. Say "measured", "repeatable", "physically similar", "not established".
