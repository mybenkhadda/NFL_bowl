## Use by role

- **Scout.** Pick a prospect, open his twins, keep only distinguishable differences, read the phase, watch that phase on film. Treat *not distinguishable* as *same*.
- **Position coach.** Read the phase and the matching NFL demand; use the suggested targeted measurement to look again. No training conclusion follows from an observed difference.
- **General manager.** Read the *new information* and *redundancy* lines. Tracking can justify additional evaluation; it is not a ranking input.

## What the Lab will not do

No grade, no ranking, no draft value, no hidden-star or bust label, no NFL forecast. Tracked Combine features did not improve held-out NFL prediction in this project (C08, C14).
